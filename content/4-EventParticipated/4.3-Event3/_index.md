# AI From Scratch

### Event Objectives

- Introduce the fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence and Generative AI.
- Present AWS AI services and demonstrate how they can be integrated into modern applications.
- Share practical knowledge from AWS engineers through real-world use cases.
- Encourage students to explore AI technologies through technical demonstrations and interactive activities.

### Speakers

- **Nguyễn Tuấn Thịnh** – DevOps/Cloud Engineer, AWS
- **Nguyễn Công Minh** – DevOps Engineer, AWS
- AWS Vietnam Specialists
- **Mai Hoàng Đỉnh** – Lecturer, IA Department, FPT University HCMC (Academic Supervisor)

### Key Highlights

#### Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

- Overview of Artificial Intelligence, Generative AI, and their growing impact on software development.
- Discussion on how cloud platforms simplify AI adoption by providing managed AI services.
- Introduction to practical AI applications that can be built on AWS.

#### AWS AI Services

- Introduction to **Amazon Bedrock** for building Generative AI applications using foundation models.
- Demonstration of a conversational AI workflow using AWS AI services:
  - **Amazon Transcribe** to convert speech into text.
  - **Amazon Translate** to translate conversations between languages.
  - **Amazon Comprehend** to analyze sentiment and identify conversation topics.
  - **Amazon Lex** to detect user intent and communicate with backend APIs or databases.
  - **Amazon Polly** to convert responses into natural-sounding speech.
- Overview of **Amazon Bedrock Knowledge Bases** and vector store options used for Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), illustrating how enterprise knowledge can be integrated into AI applications.

#### Deployment and Development Practices

- One of the technical sessions discussed application deployment and modern software development workflows.
- The speakers shared practical experiences in selecting deployment approaches based on project requirements.
- Real-world examples highlighted how AI services can be integrated into cloud-based applications.

#### Interactive Activities

- The seminar concluded with an interactive game developed by the speakers.
- Participants were invited to join the activity and experience one of the presenters' demonstration projects.
- I volunteered to participate together with one of my classmates, won the competition, and received a one-month ChatGPT Plus subscription as a prize.

### Key Takeaways

#### AI Fundamentals

- Learned how AWS AI services can be combined into a complete conversational AI workflow.
- Understood the role of Amazon Bedrock in developing Generative AI applications without managing foundation models directly.
- Gained a basic understanding of Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and how knowledge bases and vector stores support AI-powered applications.

#### Practical Learning

- Real-world demonstrations made AI concepts easier to understand than theoretical explanations alone.
- AWS managed services significantly reduce the complexity of integrating AI capabilities into software applications.
- Technical seminars provide valuable opportunities to learn directly from experienced cloud engineers.

#### Professional Growth

- Continuous learning is essential to keep up with the rapid development of AI technologies.
- Participating in technical seminars helps broaden technical knowledge while exposing students to current industry practices.
- Interactive activities encourage active participation and make learning more engaging.

### Applying to Work

- Explore Amazon Bedrock when developing AI-powered cloud applications.
- Learn more about Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and knowledge base integration.
- Experiment with AWS AI services such as Amazon Transcribe, Amazon Comprehend, Amazon Lex, and Amazon Polly in future projects.
- Continue participating in technical communities and workshops to strengthen practical cloud computing and AI skills.
