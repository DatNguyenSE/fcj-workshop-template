# FCAJ – Agentic AI Build Week 

### Event Objectives

- Share practical experiences from teams that participated in the **FCAJ – Agentic AI Build Week**.
- Learn how Agentic AI solutions were designed, developed, and deployed on AWS during the buildathon.
- Explore real-world cloud architectures and technical decisions behind the competition projects.
- Gain insights from participants about teamwork, problem-solving, and software development under time constraints.

### Speakers

- Representatives from the following FCAJ Build Week teams:
  - **One Team**
  - **Plan V**
  - **3KA**
  - **Dream AI Team**
  - **Six Pillar Team**

- Opening remarks by an international guest speaker.

### Key Highlights

#### Opening Session

The event began with an opening presentation delivered by an international guest speaker. He shared his personal journey in the technology industry and emphasized how rapidly Artificial Intelligence is evolving. Rather than focusing solely on technical knowledge, he encouraged participants to remain curious, continue learning, and embrace new technologies throughout their careers. His message highlighted that continuous improvement and adaptability are essential qualities for anyone pursuing a career in cloud computing and AI.

#### Sharing Experiences from the Build Week

The main session featured representatives from the teams that had participated in the **FCAJ – Agentic AI Build Week**. Instead of simply demonstrating finished products, each team reflected on its development journey throughout the buildathon.

The presentations covered:

- The real-world problem the team wanted to solve.
- The motivation behind the project idea.
- System architecture and AWS services selected.
- Technical challenges encountered during development.
- Lessons learned after completing the buildathon.

Several projects addressed different business scenarios. One project introduced an intelligent self-service food ordering solution where customers could place orders through an AI-assisted kiosk. Another project focused on financial services by integrating AI into customer interaction and transaction-related workflows.

Although the project domains were different, every team demonstrated how AWS managed services could be combined to build practical, scalable, and maintainable AI-powered applications.

#### Cloud Architecture Demonstrations

One of the most valuable parts of the event was the architecture walkthrough presented by each team.

Across different projects, many teams adopted modern cloud-native architectures by combining AWS services such as:

- Amazon Bedrock and AgentCore for AI reasoning and orchestration.
- Amazon API Gateway and AWS Lambda for backend services.
- Amazon SQS for asynchronous processing.
- Amazon DynamoDB and Amazon S3 for data storage.
- Amazon OpenSearch Service for search and vector storage.
- Amazon ECS, AWS Fargate, and Amazon ECR for containerized deployment.
- Amazon CloudFront, Amazon Cognito, and Application Load Balancer for secure application delivery.
- Amazon CloudWatch, AWS CloudTrail, AWS WAF, GuardDuty, IAM, AWS KMS, and Secrets Manager for monitoring and security.

These architecture presentations demonstrated that successful AI applications require not only foundation models but also scalable infrastructure, security, monitoring, storage, and orchestration services working together.

### Event Experience

Attending the **FCAJ – Agentic AI Build Week Sharing Session** gave me a clearer understanding of how an AI project evolves from an initial idea into a complete cloud-based solution within the context of a hackathon.

#### Learning from Build Week Experiences

Rather than presenting only the final products, the participating teams shared the complete journey of developing their projects during the Build Week. They discussed how they identified practical problems, designed system architectures, selected appropriate AWS services, collaborated as a team, and continuously improved their solutions throughout the competition.

These experiences helped me realize that developing an AI application involves much more than implementing machine learning models. It also requires thoughtful system design, effective teamwork, continuous iteration, and the ability to adapt to technical challenges.

#### Understanding Modern Cloud Architectures

Another aspect that impressed me was the variety of cloud architectures presented during the session. Although each team worked on a different business problem, most projects followed similar cloud-native design principles by integrating services such as Amazon Bedrock, Lambda, API Gateway, Amazon ECS, Amazon S3, DynamoDB, CloudWatch, IAM, and many other AWS managed services.

These real-world examples allowed me to better understand how AWS services can be combined to build reliable, scalable, and secure AI-powered systems.

#### Personal Reflection

The sharing session broadened my perspective on software development beyond technical implementation. Listening to the experiences of teams that had successfully completed the Build Week showed me the importance of planning, collaboration, system architecture, and continuous learning throughout the software development process.

The event also motivated me to continue strengthening my AWS knowledge and backend development skills so that I can confidently participate in similar hackathons and contribute to practical cloud-based AI projects in the future.
